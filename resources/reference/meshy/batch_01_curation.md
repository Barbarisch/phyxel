# Batch 01 - image curation record

Curated 2026-11-21 for `resources/reference/meshy/batch_01.json` (input to `tools/meshy_import.py --batch`). **No Meshy request was made and no credit was spent.**

## What was done

- Candidates: every file in the image sections of the five era dossiers (`docs/evidence/worldforge_v2/era_reference/{medieval,roman,adobe,norse,edo}.md`) that the dossier named individually, plus files found by searching Wikimedia Commons for a better image of the same building or type (search hits were filtered to free licences and a long side of at least 1200 px before download).
- Each candidate was downloaded through the Commons API (standard thumbnail renditions; Commons refused original-size downloads with HTTP 429) and **looked at**, first on a contact sheet and, for every accepted image, again at larger size. Files under 800 px on the long side were rejected on the API's size figure without being downloaded.
- Accept rule: one building dominates the frame; whole or nearly whole in a three-quarter or frontal view; no people, vehicles, scaffolding or heavy vegetation in front; daylight; at least 800 px; a licence that allows reuse and redistribution (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA).
- Accepted images were saved as JPEG, long side at most 1600 px, under `resources/reference/meshy/<era>/source_images/`. **10 of them are cropped** to remove a person, vehicle or wire at the edge of the frame; the crop is recorded in the manifest note. Cropping is the only edit - nothing was retouched. A cropped copy is a derivative: CC BY-SA sources keep their licence and need the credit line below.
- Sizes: see the size column. `SOURCED` = a page-read dimension of that building in the dossier. `NOMINAL` = the dossier has no overall dimension for that building; the value is a type figure taken from another building, or a labelled placeholder where the dossier has nothing at all. Nominal sizes give proportions only.
