# kitchen — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: dwellings (mid+ status), tavern, inn, manor, hall_house.

## Function
Cook + prepare food. Service tier. (Lower-status dwellings cook on the hall hearth — no separate kitchen.)

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A **cooking station** — a cook-hearth or an oven (the function-definer; no cooking station = not a kitchen, T1).

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- Work table / dresser-board, shelving, barrels/crocks, a pot-rack, a wash basin.

## Service
- Cooking station **vented** (chimney #14); a work surface beside it; **water access**; **fire-safe** (often a detached kitchen in a manor — fire).

## Adjacency
- Near the hall (service it) but fire-separated; near the pantry/buttery + the well; the rear in a townhouse.

## Dimensions
- `to_ground` (no clean medieval kitchen-room standard); ceiling ≥ 2.134 m (Part 5).

## Function testers
- **F1** A cooking station (cook-hearth/oven) — vented.
- **F2** A work surface beside it.
- **F3** Water access; food storage adjacent.
- **F4** Fire-safe (vented; detached/separated where a big fire).

## Grounding ledger
- Cooking station + venting — Part 3 / the bakery & hall sheets; **room size `to_ground`**; ceiling — Part 5.
