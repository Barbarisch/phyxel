## Function
House cattle — stalls + feed + muck drainage.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- **Stalls** sized to cattle.

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- A hayrack, a feed/water trough, a hayloft above.

## Service
- A **muck channel/drain** running **downhill** to a midden; ventilation; feed + water.

## Adjacency
- Stalls in rows facing a **feed passage**; the byre **downhill** of the dwelling (longhouse — across the cross-passage).

## Dimensions
- `to_ground` (stall size per beast).

## Function testers
- **F1** Cattle stalls + a feed passage.
- **F2** A muck drain running away (downhill, from water + the dwelling).
- **F3** Hay storage; ventilation.
