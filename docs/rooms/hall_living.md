# hall / living — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: croft, longhouse, hall_house, manor_hall, tavern, most dwellings.

## Function
The household's main room — gather, eat, (lower status) cook + sleep — around the hearth. Public/semi-public.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A **hearth/fireplace** (the heat + light + social centre).

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- Trestle table, benches/settle, stools, a chest, a wall light; (open-hall) a dais.

## Service
- Hearth **vented** (chimney #14, or a smoke louvre in an open hall); seating faces the hearth/table.

## Adjacency
- The heart of the plan; off the entrance/cross-passage; the service end one side, the private end the other (hall_house/manor).

## Dimensions
- From the bay model — REUSE `room_program.json` (hall = 2 bays in a hall_house; the great hall 1.5–3× long:wide in a manor). Ceiling ≥ 2.134 m (Part 5).

## Function testers
- **F1** A hearth present + vented.
- **F2** Seating + a table; space to gather.
- **F3** Faces the hearth/table; clear of the door swing.
