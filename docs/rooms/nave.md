# nave — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: church, chapel, cathedral. Reuses the `temple` archetype sheet.

## Function
The **laity's** worship space — congregation, facing the altar. Public (T0).

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- Congregation space (pews/standing) **facing the altar**; a **font** near the entrance.

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- Aisles (arcade), a pulpit; (later) benches/pews.

## Service
- Oriented to the chancel/altar; daylight (clerestory windows).

## Adjacency
- **West of the screen** (the chancel is east); the entrance at the **west/south porch**; separated from the chancel by the **rood screen**.

## Dimensions
- Nave **≈ 2 bays wide**, bay-divided (cited, `temple`); proportions `to_ground`.

## Function testers
- **F1** Congregation space facing the altar (oriented east).
- **F2** A font near the entrance.
- **F3** Separated from the chancel by a screen/step.
