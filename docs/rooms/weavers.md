## Function
Weave cloth on the loom. **Strong light** is the defining requirement.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A **loom**.

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- A spinning wheel/distaff, cloth/wool storage.

## Service
- **Strong natural light** (a window wall); **warp clearance** (room to work the warp).

## Adjacency
- The **best-lit side** (big / north windows); a dry, secure cloth store nearby.

## Dimensions
- Loom **1.5–2.0 m tall × ≤ weaver's arm span** (~0.6–1.0 m); a **24 yd** warp (a "piece") to work — both cited (`tailor_weaver`). Room length for the warp `to_ground`.

## Function testers
- **F1** A loom present.
- **F2** **Strong natural light** at the loom.
- **F3** Warp clearance (room long enough).
- **F4** A dry, secure cloth store.
