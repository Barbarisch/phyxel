# barn interior — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: barn (tithe/great). Reuses the `barn` archetype sheet.

## Function
Store + process the harvest — a **threshing floor** between the great doors + aisled storage.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A central **threshing floor** between **opposed great doors** (the winnowing cross-draught).

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- Aisled storage bays (sheaves/hay), a hay mow.

## Service
- A **high vented roof** (dry); cart-width opposed doors; raised off damp ground (plinths).

## Adjacency
- The threshing floor **midway** between the opposed doors; aisles either side; near the fields/yard; away from fire.

## Dimensions
- Aisled clear span; the whole barn **30–50 m × 10–15 m**, ~9–14 bays (cited, `barn`).

## Function testers
- **F1** A central threshing floor between **opposed great doors** (cross-draught).
- **F2** Cart-width doors; aisled storage either side.
- **F3** A high, vented, dry roof.
