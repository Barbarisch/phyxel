# chancel / sanctuary — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: church, chapel, cathedral. Reuses the `temple` archetype sheet.

## Function
The **clergy's** sacred space — the altar + the rite. The holy (east) end. Clergy (T1).

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- An **altar** at the **east end**, raised + lit.

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- A lectern, sedilia, a piscina, choir stalls; (cathedral) the cathedra.

## Service
- At the **east end**; **raised + emphasized** (a window/light above the altar); beyond the rood screen.

## Adjacency
- **East of the screen**; the sacristy opens off it (secure); the nave (laity) is west.

## Dimensions
- `to_ground`; the altar at the axis terminus.

## Function testers
- **F1** An altar at the east (axis) end.
- **F2** Raised + emphasized (light/window).
- **F3** Beyond the screen (clergy-only); a secure sacristy off it.
