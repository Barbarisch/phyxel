## Systems Reference Document 5.1 (Wizards of the Coast)

The monster stat blocks in `resources/monsters/` (including every
`srd_*.json` file, ingested by `tools/ingest_srd_monsters.py`) and the
creature names/statistics referenced by the bestiary rigs
(`tools/creature_forge/bestiary.json`, `resources/monsters/visuals/`)
derive from the **System Reference Document 5.1** by Wizards of the Coast
LLC, available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document.

All creature *visuals* (the voxel rigs in `resources/animated_characters/`)
are original Phyxel work generated by `tools/creature_forge/` and
`tools/anim_pipeline/derive_humanoid_variant.py`.
