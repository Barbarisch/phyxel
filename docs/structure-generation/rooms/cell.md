# cell / dungeon cell — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: gaol, castle dungeon, town_hall lock-up. Reuses the `gaol` archetype sheet.

## Function
Secure confinement — hold a prisoner against escape. Most-secure.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A **barred / locked door**.

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- A wall ring / manacles, straw, a slop bucket; a small high aperture.

## Service
- Opens **only onto controlled interior space** (no exterior door/large window); a tiny, high, **body-impassable** aperture.

## Adjacency
- Off a controlled internal passage, never the exterior; felons separated from debtors; (dungeon) under the keep.

## Dimensions
- A felon cell ~**3.0–6.4 m × 2.0–3.4 m**; a single improved cell **3.05 × 2.03 m**; aperture **~0.6 × 0.3 m** — cited (`gaol`).

## Function testers
- **F1** A barred/locked door, opening only onto controlled space.
- **F2** The aperture is body-impassable (≤ ~0.6 × 0.3 m) and high.
- **F3** No exterior door/large window.

## Grounding ledger
- Cell + aperture dims — REUSE `gaol` (Lancaster/Southampton, theprison.org.uk, cited).
