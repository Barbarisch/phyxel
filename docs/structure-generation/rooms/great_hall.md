## Function
The lord's public hall — receive, dine, hold court. The seat of lordship. Public.

- A **hearth** + a **high table** + benches.

- Heated (hearth + flue); the **lord's high table at the dais end**; the service end behind the screen.

## Adjacency
- The main floor of the keep (the **first-floor entrance** level); the great chamber/solar above (private); service behind the screens.
