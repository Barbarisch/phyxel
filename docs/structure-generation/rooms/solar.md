## Function
The family's **private living/sleeping** room — withdrawn from the hall. The high (private) end, usually upper.

- A **bed** + a **fireplace** (a heated private chamber).

## Service
- **Heated** (its own fireplace + flue #14); **well-lit** (a good window / oriel); private.

## Adjacency
- **Beyond the dais** (the high end), often over a parlour/service; the most private room — deep in the plan, upper floor.
