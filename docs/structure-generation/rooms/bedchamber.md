## Function
Sleep + private retreat. Private tier.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A **bed**.

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- A **chest** (the medieval "wardrobe/dresser"), a stool, a candle/rushlight, a chamber pot, a washstand (basin + ewer).

## Service
- A **light source** (candle/window); a **door for privacy**.

## Adjacency
- Private — off a stair/passage, away from the public hall; the door doesn't open onto the street.
