# servants' quarters — Room Data Sheet

> Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Used by: manor, inn, tavern, larger households.

## Function
Sleeping space for servants/staff. Service tier — plainer + separate from the family.

## Required fixtures (→ #17)
- A **pallet / simple bed** (a place to sleep).

## Typical fixtures (→ #16)
- A chest, a stool, a rushlight.

## Service
- A light source; basic.

## Adjacency
- **Separate from the family apartments** — over the service end, in the attic/garret, or off the kitchen/yard; near the work they do.

## Dimensions
- Minimal — `to_ground`; ceiling ≥ 2.032 m (non-habitable storage floor often, Part 5).

## Function testers
- **F1** A place to sleep (pallet/bed).
- **F2** **Separate** from the family apartments.
- **F3** Plainer than the family rooms (status reads down).
