# 59 · apply_setting_overlay

> Tier: Fantasy. Part-1 status: **M**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). The final placer.

## Job
Overlay the **world bible** onto the grounded archetypes — arcane programs, deity iconography, race material
palettes, magical lighting — and **flag** bible-licensed impossibilities as engine gaps.

## Reads
- The world bible (#58); the built/derived structure (a grounded archetype); Part 9 CC.

## Emits
- The fantasy overlay: a wizard tower's **arcane rooms**, a temple's **deity iconography**, a race's **material palette/form**, **magical lighting** (`glow`); **flags** for impossibilities (non-Euclidean / floating).

## Algorithm
1. Take the **grounded** structure (Parts 5–8).
2. Apply the bible overlays (arcane program, iconography, race palette, magic materials → `glow`).
3. Where the bible licenses the impossible (bigger-inside, floating), **FLAG** as an engine gap (CC8) — don't fake.

## Satisfies (checks)
CC3 (grounded skeleton + overlay), CC4 (race architecture), CC5 (pantheon iconography), CC6 (magic materials map to real), CC7 (genre honesty), CC8 (engine-gap honesty).

## Engine capability needed
- Material/program overlay — ⚠️; `glow` — ✅; **non-Euclidean / floating** — ❌ (flag, not faked).

## Failure modes
- A physics-defying structure **faked** (CC8); iconography wrong per deity (CC5); magic materials invented vs `glow` (CC6).

## Function testers
- **F1** The overlay sits on a **grounded skeleton** (CC3).
- **F2** Race/pantheon correct per the bible (CC4/CC5).
- **F3** Magic materials **map to real** engine materials (`glow`, CC6).
- **F4** Impossibilities **flagged, not faked** (CC8).

## Grounding
- Bible-sourced (Part 9 / #58); `glow` material real; the structure stays grounded (Parts 5–8).
