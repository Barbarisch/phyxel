# Tanner / Tanyard — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). **The archetypal noxious trade (Z3).**

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `tanner`
- **function:** turn raw hides into leather
- **aka:** tanyard, tannery; **currier** (the finishing trade)
- **group:** Commerce (Part 6) — **noxious**
- **extends:** an **open tanyard of pits** (NOT a townhouse shell)
- **genre/period:** real.

## 2. Essence
An **open yard of tan pits** through which hides pass in increasing tannin strength over **months** — the
foulest trade, defined by its pits, water, bark mill, drying sheds, and its **banishment to the town edge**.

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Stench + toxic waste** (urine, lime, dung, rotting flesh) — must be at the **edge, downwind + downstream, by running water**.
- **Water** — pits + washing need a stream/leat.
- The **worst neighbour** in any settlement.

## 4. Access / zoning — a **process flow**, not a privacy gradient
raw-hide receipt → soak/wash → **lime pits** (dehair) → **tan pits** (increasing liquor) → **drying sheds** →
currier's finishing. Plus a **bark store + mill**.

## 5. Required spaces (program)
| Space | Purpose | Required fixtures | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| lime_pits | dehair/soften | pits | each ~**2 m dia × 1 m deep** (cited) |
| tan_pits | tan (increasing liquor) | rows of pits, oak-/stone-lined | ~2 m dia × 1 m deep; **many**, in process order |
| wash area | clean + flush waste | a stream/leat | on running water |
| bark store + mill | tannin source | stacked oak bark, a bark mill | `to_ground` |
| drying sheds | dry the leather | slatted, airy sheds + racks | airy |
| beam shed / hut | scraping + an office | a beam, tools | `to_ground` |

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. **On a stream/leat** — water in, waste out.
2. Pits in **process order** (lime → weak → strong tannin liquor).
3. **Drying sheds airy + downwind.**
4. The whole yard at the **town edge, downwind + downstream** of dwellings + the water intake (Z3).
5. Tanners **cluster** (a tanyard quarter, typically by the river).

## 7. Construction & materials
- An **open yard**; pits dug + lined (oak plank / brick / stone); open **slatted drying sheds**; a bark mill (horse/water powered).
- WANTED: tan-pit, drying-rack/shed, bark mill, hide props.

## 8. Signature / legibility
**Rows of foul pits**; stacked oak bark; hides on drying racks/slatted sheds; the unmistakable **stench**; at the
river's edge, apart from the town.

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a single tanner's few pits.
- **Mid:** a tanyard (many pits + sheds + mill).
- **Up:** a **tanners' quarter** — clustered along the river.

## 10. Function testers
- **F1** Multiple tan pits (~2 m dia × 1 m deep) in **process order** (lime → increasing tannin).
- **F2** A water source (stream/leat) feeding + flushing the pits.
- **F3** Drying sheds (airy).
- **F4** A bark store + mill.
- **F5** Sited at the town **edge, downwind + downstream** of dwellings + water intake (Z3).
- **F6** Ideally clustered with other tanners (a tanyard quarter).
