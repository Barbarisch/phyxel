# Barn (Tithe / Great Barn) — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md).

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `barn`
- **function:** store + process the harvest (threshing, stacking sheaves, hay)
- **aka:** tithe barn, great barn, grange barn
- **group:** Agriculture & outbuildings (Part 6)
- **extends:** a great aisled timber shed
- **genre/period:** real (medieval monastic/tithe barns).

## 2. Essence
A **great aisled shed with a central threshing floor + big opposed doors**, storing sheaves + hay and processing
the harvest. Defining quality: the clear aisled volume + the threshing floor + cart-width opposed doors.

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Fire** — dry crop (keep away from open flame).
- **Damp** — a high-pitched vented roof, off the ground.
- **Span** — aisled to clear the threshing floor.
- **Cart access** — big doors.

## 4. Access / zoning — a working volume
Opposed great doors (a through-passage = the threshing floor, in the draught) + aisled storage bays + a hay mow.

## 5. Required spaces (program)
| Space | Purpose | Required fixtures | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| threshing floor | thresh + winnow | a clear floor between **opposed doors** (cross-draught) | central |
| storage bays | sheaves/hay | aisled bays | either side |
| great doors | cart access | **cart-width opposed doors** | midway |
| (store end) | grain/tools | bins | optional |

**Typical:** 30–50 m long × 10–15 m wide; aisled (clear span) or cruck; ~9–14 bays.

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. **Opposed great doors midway** — the threshing floor sits in the **cross-draught** (winnowing).
2. **Aisles for stacking** either side.
3. A **high vented roof** (dry).
4. Near the fields + farmyard; **away from fire**.

## 7. Construction & materials
- **Aisled** (arcade posts) or **cruck**, timber, on **stone plinths**; a very high-pitched roof (thatch/tile/slate) for ventilation; big opposed doors; clear span via aisles.
- WANTED: aisle posts, great doors, threshing floor.

## 8. Signature / legibility
A huge, long, **high-roofed aisled shed with great opposed doors**; a cathedral-like timber interior; dominates
the farmstead.

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a small 3-bay barn.
- **Up:** a great tithe/monastic barn (Great Coxwell, Harmondsworth — 40–50 m, many bays).

## 10. Function testers
- **F1** A clear aisled storage volume (30–50 m × 10–15 m typical; aisled or cruck for clear span).
- **F2** A central threshing floor between **opposed great doors** (the winnowing draught).
- **F3** Cart-width opposed doors.
- **F4** A high-pitched, vented roof (keep grain/hay dry).
- **F5** Raised off damp ground (plinths).
- **F6** Sited near the fields, away from fire.
