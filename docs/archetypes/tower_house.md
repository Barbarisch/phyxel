# Tower House / Wizard Tower — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Dimensions reuse Part 6 (auditor-confirmed).

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `tower_house` / `wizard_tower`
- **function:** a compact fortified residence (tower_house) / a mage's tower (wizard_tower)
- **aka:** peel tower, bastle, tower keep
- **group:** Power / fortified (Part 6)
- **extends:** a stacked vertical keep
- **genre/period:** tower_house = real; **wizard_tower = tower_house + a fantasy overlay** (Part 9).

## 2. Essence
A **compact, tall, thick-walled tower** — stacked single rooms over a vaulted store, entered at **first-floor
for defence**, topped by a parapet. Defining quality: verticality + thick walls + a defensible raised entrance
(+ wizard: an arcane program overlaid).

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Raid** (the tower_house's reason — a border refuge) → the first-floor entrance + thick walls + few low openings + a parapet.
- *(Wizard)* magical threats / secrecy → a warded top, a guarded lab.

## 4. Access tiers / zoning
- **T0** ground vault — storage, **no external door** (entered from above).
- **First-floor ENTRANCE** (a removable stair/ladder) → the hall.
- **T1** stacked private chambers above.
- **T2** the parapet/roof (defence/lookout).
- *(Wizard)* the lab/observatory at the top.

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. The **entrance is at first-floor level** (a removable stair/ladder — defence).
2. The **ground vault has no exterior door** (a hatch from the hall above).
3. Rooms **stack** via a **mural/spiral stair** in the wall thickness.
4. A **parapet/wall-walk** on top; a **barmkin** (walled yard) around the base.

## 7. Construction & materials
- Stone, **thick walls** (grounded: **6 ft / 1.83 m below the vault, 4 ft / 1.22 m above**; round Balief **2.54 m** walls).
- **Grounded minimum 6.1 × 4.9 × 12.2 m** (Henry VI 1429 statute); a vaulted ground floor; few small openings low, larger higher; a parapet.
- *(Wizard)* + arcane materials / the `glow` material. Reuse Part 6 dims.

## 8. Signature / legibility
A **tall, narrow, thick stone tower** with a first-floor door, small low windows, a parapet, in a barmkin.
*(Wizard:* a glowing top, arcane sigils.)*

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a small peel/bastle.
- **Up:** a tower house (3–5 stories) → a `keep`.
- **Wizard:** tower_house + lab/library/observatory/summoning circle (Part 9 overlay) — BG3 Ramazith's Tower.
