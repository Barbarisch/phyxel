# Byre / Cowshed — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Reuses the `longhouse` byre.

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `byre` / `cowshed`
- **function:** house cattle
- **aka:** cowshed, shippon, cow-house
- **group:** Agriculture & outbuildings (Part 6)
- **extends:** a stalled shed (the `longhouse` byre, freestanding)
- **genre/period:** real.

## 2. Essence
A **stalled shed for cattle with feed and a muck drain** — the longhouse byre, freestanding. Defining quality:
stalls sized to cattle + feed + muck-out drainage.

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Muck** — drainage downhill, away from water + the dwelling.
- **Cattle health** — ventilation, dryness.
- **Feed** — a hayrack/loft.

## 4. Access / zoning — a working shed
Stalls + a feed passage + a muck drain + a hay store/loft.

## 5. Required spaces (program)
| Space | Purpose | Required fixtures | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| stalls | hold cattle | tethering/stalls sized to beasts, a hayrack | `to_ground` (stall size) |
| feed passage | feed | a passage + trough | — |
| muck channel | drain | a sloped drain to a midden | downhill |
| hay loft/store | feed store | a loft / end bay | above/end |

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. **Stalls in rows facing a feed passage.**
2. A **muck channel runs behind/downhill to a midden** (away from water + the dwelling).
3. Hay above/at the end; ventilated.
4. *(Longhouse)* across the cross-passage from the dwelling, downhill.

## 7. Construction & materials
- Timber/stone, a low shed; a **drained (sloped) floor**; a hayloft; thatch/tile.
- WANTED: cattle stalls, hayrack, feed trough.

## 8. Signature / legibility
A **long low shed** with a muck drain + a hayloft; near the farmstead.

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a few stalls (a croft's cow).
- **Up:** a byre → a great monastic/manorial cattle range.

## 10. Function testers
- **F1** Cattle stalls sized to the beasts.
- **F2** Feed (a hayrack/trough + a feed passage).
- **F3** A muck channel/drain running away (downhill, away from water + the dwelling).
- **F4** Hay storage (loft/end).
- **F5** Ventilation.
