# Dovecote — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md).

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `dovecote`
- **function:** raise pigeons/doves — squabs (meat), eggs, and dung (fertiliser/saltpetre)
- **aka:** culverhouse, columbarium, pigeon house
- **group:** Agriculture & outbuildings (Part 6) — often a **lord's privilege**
- **extends:** a circular stone tower lined with nest niches
- **genre/period:** real.

## 2. Essence
A (usually **circular**) stone building **lined with tiers of nest niches**, with a flight aperture and a
revolving ladder (**potence**) to reach them. Defining quality: the niche-lined walls + the flight hole + the
potence access.

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Vermin / predators** — thick walls, niches high, a single controlled door.
- **Collecting squabs** — the potence reaches every niche.
- **The birds' access** — flight apertures.
- **(Legally)** a lord's privilege.

## 4. Access / zoning
A single low human door + **flight apertures** (a roof louvre/cupola or high wall holes) for the birds; the
**potence** (a central revolving pole + ladder) reaches every niche.

## 5. Required spaces (program)
| Space | Purpose | Required fixtures | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| chamber | house the birds | walls lined with **tiers of nest niches** (boulins) | circular |
| nest niches (boulins) | breeding | one pair each; **hundreds** (e.g. 580) | ~**30–40 cm deep × 20–25 cm wide** |
| potence | reach the niches | a central **revolving ladder** | centre |
| flight aperture | birds in/out | a louvre/cupola or wall holes | high/roof |
| human door | access | a single low door | — |

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. **Niches in tiers all around the interior walls.**
2. A **central potence** to reach them (circular plan).
3. **Flight holes high** (a roof louvre/cupola or upper wall).
4. A **single low human door** (predator/theft control).
5. Set in the farmyard / manor close.

## 7. Construction & materials
- Circular **stone**, **metre-thick walls**; tiers of nest niches; a pointed/conical roof with a flight
  louvre/cupola; a potence.
- WANTED: nest niches, potence, the dovecote shell.

## 8. Signature / legibility
A **round stone tower with a conical roof + a flight louvre**, hundreds of niches inside, a revolving ladder.

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a small gable dovecote (a few niches).
- **Up:** a great circular columbarium (500+ niches + a potence — Kinwarton 580).

## 10. Function testers
- **F1** Tiers of nest niches lining the walls (boulins ~30–40 × 20–25 cm, one pair each; hundreds).
- **F2** A flight aperture (louvre/cupola/holes) for the birds.
- **F3** A means to reach all niches (a **potence** in a circular plan, or accessible tiers).
- **F4** A single controlled human door (predator/theft).
- **F5** *(circular)* the potence geometry works.
