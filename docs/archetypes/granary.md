# Granary — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md).

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `granary`
- **function:** store threshed grain — dry and rat-proof
- **aka:** granary, hórreo, grain store
- **group:** Agriculture & outbuildings (Part 6)
- **extends:** a raised timber store on staddle stones
- **genre/period:** real.

## 2. Essence
A small square timber store **raised on staddle stones** — air beneath keeps grain dry, mushroom caps keep
vermin out. Defining quality: the **raised, vermin-proof, ventilated dry store**.

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Vermin** (rats/mice) → staddle stones with smooth overhanging caps.
- **Damp** → raised, air beneath.
- **Theft** → lockable. **Fire.**

## 4. Access / zoning
A raised store reached by a (removable) step/ladder; bins inside for separate crops.

## 5. Required spaces (program)
| Space | Purpose | Required fixtures | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| granary box | store grain | grain bins (2–3 crops) | square, single-storey timber; **500–2500 bushels** |
| staddle stones | raise + rat-proof | **12 stones (4 × 3 grid)**, ~**600 mm tall**, smooth overhanging caps | under the box |
| access step | get in | a removable/awkward step | — |

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. **Raised ~600 mm on staddle stones** (air + vermin-proof) with **smooth overhanging caps** (un-climbable).
2. A **removable/awkward access step** (vermin can't bridge).
3. Near the barn/farmyard; dry, ventilated.

## 7. Construction & materials
- Timber-framed square box on **12 (4 × 3) staddle stones**; slate/tile roof; lockable.
- WANTED: staddle stones, grain bins, access step.

## 8. Signature / legibility
A small **square timber building standing on mushroom-shaped stones**, a little ladder up; in the farmyard.

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a small granary (~500 bushels).
- **Up:** a larger one (~2500) → a great monastic/manorial grain store (a tower / over an undercroft).

## 10. Function testers

- **F2** Air circulation beneath (dry).
