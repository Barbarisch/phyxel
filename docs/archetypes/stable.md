# Stable — Archetype Data Sheet

> Status: **DRAFT**. Schema: [`README.md`](README.md). Reuses the Part 3 stable program.

## 1. Identity
- **id:** `stable`
- **function:** house + tend horses
- **aka:** livery (commercial), mews
- **group:** Agriculture & outbuildings (Part 6)
- **extends:** a stalled shed + a tack room + a yard
- **genre/period:** real.

## 2. Essence
A **shed of horse stalls with hay/feed/water + tack storage + a muck-out**, on a yard. Defining quality: stalls
sized for horses + feed/water + tack + muck-out.

## 3. Threat model / failure modes
- **Muck** — drainage/midden.
- **Feed/water** — daily.
- **Fire** — hay + a lamp (keep fire away from hay).
- **Horse health** — ventilation, dry.

## 4. Access / zoning — a working shed
Stalls + a feed/tack passage + a hay loft + a muck-out + the yard; (inn/great house) a groom's quarters.

## 5. Required spaces (program)
| Space | Purpose | Required fixtures | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| stalls | hold horses | standing or box stalls, sized for horses | `to_ground` (reason ~1.5–1.8 m standing) |
| feed | feed + water | hayrack, feed + water troughs | — |
| tack room | store gear | saddle/harness racks (dry, secure) | secure |
| hay loft | feed store | a loft above | above |
| muck-out + midden | dispose | a barrow, a midden | — |
| yard | handle horses | open space | — |

## 6. Adjacency & circulation rules
1. **Stalls in rows off a passage.**
2. The **tack room is dry + secure** (valuable gear).
3. A **hay loft above**; water + a muck-out.
4. The **yard** for handling; near the dwelling/inn it serves.
5. **Hay/fire separation** (hay away from lamps/forge).

## 7. Construction & materials
- Timber/stone shed; a drained floor; a hayloft; a tack room.
- WANTED: horse stalls, hayrack, troughs, tack racks.

## 8. Signature / legibility
A **row of stall doors + a hayloft + a muck heap**; a yard; near an inn/manor.

## 9. Status / period / setting scaling
- **Down:** a couple of stalls.
- **Up:** a stable + tack + loft → a great manorial/coaching-inn stable (a galleried yard).
- **Fantasy:** a stable for exotic mounts.
