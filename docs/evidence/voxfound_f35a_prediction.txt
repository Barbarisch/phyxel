Read: a size-8 brush is block-aligned, so it edits ONE chunk; the other 1-3 are rebuilt only because their border
plane was written. Each of them pays three passes over its WHOLE face list (planRunsDelta scans every face;
applyClusteredDelta copies every face one by one; the pool rewrites its whole face span, ~300 KB) whatever changed.

Changes (all exact by construction; each pinned by a test shown red first):
A. A neighbour is rebuilt only when a border bit actually flips (Stone over Dirt, or air over air, flips none).
B. A rebuilt chunk whose face delta is empty as a multiset (removed faces = added faces), whose sprigs are unchanged
   and whose connectivity is unchanged keeps its lists untouched and is left out of the assemble (Stats.changed);
   verify still rebuilds every chunk in Stats.rebuilt, so a wrong skip would show as a mismatch.
C. planRunsDelta through the direction groups: only the faces of groups whose box meets the edit box grown by 2 are
   examined (a face that qualifies lies inside its group's box) -- the same plan (removed, slices) as today.
D. applyClusteredDelta copies an untouched group as one block instead of face by face.
Not in this increment (the plan's owed items): the pool span copy of a chunk whose faces DID change (a span rewrite
proportional to the change needs a gapped layout), the 0.36 ms connectivity flood (incremental connectivity).
