WHAT F5.1 BUILDS (engine/*/core/voxfound/Bodies.*, CPU reference; tested in VoxFoundBodiesTest)
  extractBodies(world, fractureResult, hit, mats) -> {bodies, crumbs}:
  - every voxel of a piece of kind Body or Crumb is removed from the world grid; Anchored pieces stay untouched;
  - a Body is a small grid (the piece's bounding box) + origin in world voxels; voxels keep material, part and
    damage; mass, centre of mass and inertia tensor (about the centre, world axes) from the voxels: each voxel a
    uniform cube of side v (the grid's voxelM), m = density * v^3, I_voxel = m v^2 / 6 * Id + parallel axis;
  - a Crumb keeps position, material, part, damage: counted, never deleted (F6 turns them into powder);
  - the hit's momentum P = hit.impulse * hit.dir (new Hit field, N s) is shared over the bodies:
      w_i = m_i * 1 / (1 + (d_i / 0.25 m)^2), d_i = distance from the hit to the body's nearest voxel centre,
      dp_i = P * w_i / sum w; applied at that nearest voxel (ties: lowest grid index), so
      v_i = dp_i / m_i, omega_i = I_i^-1 ((x_i - c_i) x dp_i).
    This is a modelling choice (the same falloff as the stress, §6.4), not a measured law; what is tested is that
    momentum is conserved and that the split is deterministic. Crumbs get no momentum in F5.1 (F6).
  - densities (kg/m^3, typical handbook values): Stone 2600, Bricks 1900, Wood 700, Glass 2500, WoodItem 700.

PREDICTIONS
  P1 conservation, exact: on every §8 rig over the F4.1 energy sweep and on 300 random hits, per material
     world_before = world_after + sum(body voxels) + crumbs; and voxel-for-voxel: every removed voxel appears in
     exactly one body or crumb at the same world position with the same material, part and damage.
  P2 the world after: no voxel of a Body / Crumb piece remains; every Anchored voxel is unchanged (material, part,
     fixed, damage). Body count = Body pieces; crumb count = voxels of Crumb pieces.
  P3 mass properties: a free 8 x 4 x 2-voxel stone box (nothing fixed, energy 0: one loose body) gives
     m = 64 * 2600 * 0.125^3 = 325 kg, the centre at the box centre, I = m (b^2 + c^2) / 12 per axis, off-diagonals
     0, relative error <= 1e-9. Every body on the rigs equals an independent brute-force sum (double) to 1e-9.
  P4 momentum: sum of m_i v_i = P to 1e-9 relative whenever there is at least one body; I_i omega_i =
     (x_i - c_i) x dp_i to 1e-9; two runs equal bit for bit.
  P5 churn: a stone wall hit 20 times in a row (each hit on the world left by the previous extraction, damage kept),
     conservation exact after EVERY hit; the world never gains a voxel.
  P6 cost (CPU, the F4.3 large wall 256 x 128 x 4, at the energies giving ~100 and ~1,000 loose pieces):
     extraction 1-6 ms (one pass over the grid + one over the loose voxels). Reported, not gated.
