F2.15d prediction (written 2026-11-14, before any code) -- §4.6 item 8 continued: a cheaper caster closure.

Idea. The main mesh hides a solid face next to a leaf (card) voxel. The closed casters of F2.15b re-meshed every chunk
with leaves as air: x2.31 the faces, because branches deep inside the canopies get all their faces. A light ray reaches
a face behind a leaf voxel only through the cards of every leaf voxel between it and the air. So: add ONLY the hidden
solid faces whose leaf neighbour lies within D leaf voxels of air (leaf depth, 6-connected; 1 = touches air), on top
of the main faces (not a replacement). D = infinity is the F2.15b closure (as a set of unit faces). Knob: D.

Red test first: main faces + seam faces (D = inf) = the leaves-as-air mesh as sets of unit faces, and main and seam
never share a face; at D = 1 a solid face behind a buried leaf is not added, one behind a leaf touching air is.

Predictions on the gate region (LOD off, the same 5 poses, F2.15b scripts):
1. Seam faces: D = inf ~ 0.55-0.7 M (closed - main); D = 1: 5-15 % of that (30-100 k); D = 2: 10-25 %.
2. Leaks vs shadow_mdi (px > 8 levels brighter): D = 1 at most 20 px per pose (F2.15b main+test 3-261,
   closed 4-12). forest_floor_open keeps its ~216 px terminator shift (not a leak, same as closed).
3. Faces cast after the light test: main+test (62.7 %) + half the seam.
4. Whole frame, D = 1 + light test vs the default per-chunk casters: -0.6..-1.2 ms at all 5 poses (main+test was
   -0.85..-1.4; the seam adds <= 0.3 ms).
5. If 2 and 4 hold: default ON for shadow_dir_cull with the seam at D = 1.
