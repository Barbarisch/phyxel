F5.4 -- the rest / bake-in hybrid (plan D6, §6.7 "Rest", F5 row "the rest/bake-in hybrid"; gate: conservation exact,
the visual pop measured)
Written 2026-11-18 BEFORE any F5.4 code.

Plan text: "(c) hybrid (decided, D6): keep it as a body while it is near the player or recently moved; bake it into the
world grid when it is asleep and the body budget is exceeded, farthest first. Gate: conservation exact, and the visual
pop measured." Risk 5: "baked pieces shift by up to half a voxel".

WHAT F5.4 BUILDS
  Core (engine/*/core/voxfound/BodyRest.*, CPU; tested in VoxFoundBodyRestTest):
  - planBake(body, world, other bodies) -> the voxels' new world cells, a BIJECTION body voxel -> cell that was air
    and is not inside another body. Two paths:
      rigid: the body's rotation is within 10 degrees of one of the 24 axis rotations -> bake with that rotation;
        every voxel centre then has the same fractional part, so floor() maps the grid rigidly: the piece's shape is
        exact (rotated by a multiple of 90 degrees), shifted by at most half a voxel per axis plus the 10-degree
        snap. Used when every target cell is free.
      resample: otherwise -- every voxel takes the nearest free cell to its true centre: first its own cell (the
        cell containing the centre; contested cells go to the nearest voxel), then the voxels left over take free
        cells within 2 voxels by a global greedy on distance (ties by voxel then cell index). A voxel with no free cell in reach, or a cell
        outside the world grid, refuses the WHOLE body (it stays a body; counted).
    Each voxel keeps material, part and damage. fixed = 0.
  - applyBake writes the cells; BodySimRef::remove(indices) drops baked bodies, keeping every survivor's warm start
    (the constraint keys are rebuilt with the new indices).
  - RestPolicy (the D6 hybrid; tier knobs: budget, keep radius, rest ticks): when bodies > budget, the eligible ones
    (asleep for >= restTicks, farther than keepRadius from the player) bake farthest first, ties by index, until
    bodies <= budget. Bodies resting on a baked body (AABB within one voxel) are woken so they settle on the new
    world cells. Everything not baked while over budget is counted by reason (near / awake / recent / refused).
  - SimBody gains asleepTicks (counted while asleep, reset on wake) -- the "recently moved" clock.
  Live (editor): the F5.3 route POST /api/debug/voxfound_bodies gains {"rest":{"budget","keep_m","rest_ticks"}},
    the player = the camera; a bake rewrites the preview area (today's FaceBuilderGpu path) and compacts the body
    face array by copying ranges (no re-meshing: faces still built once per body). It reports per-material counts
    of world + bodies + crumbs.
  Provenance: hand-built lab rigs (FractureLab §8), pieces from F5.1, motion from F5.2a's CPU solver; no generator.

PREDICTIONS (unit, CPU)
  R1 conservation exact: the multiset of (material, part, damage) over world + bodies + crumbs is identical after
     EVERY tick of (a) the stone wall blast (8000 N s) on a padded floor, 900 ticks, budget 0, keep radius 0,
     rest ticks 30 (everything bakes once asleep), and (b) the large wall E/T 2500 (~950 bodies) on a padded floor,
     200 ticks, budget 256. No baked cell was solid before (0 overwritten). At the end of (a) every body asleep
     long enough has baked (bodies left = refused + still awake/recent, all counted).
  R2 the policy: on hand-made scenes (bodies at known distances, asleep/awake/recent): the baked set is exactly the
     farthest eligible bodies needed to reach the budget; never a body inside the keep radius, awake, or asleep
     < rest ticks; with nothing eligible nothing bakes and the reasons are counted.
  R3 shape: a hand-made L-shaped body turned 90 degrees about y and offset by (0.3, 0.0, 0.7) voxels bakes RIGID:
     cells = the body rotated exactly, max shift <= sqrt(3)/2 voxel. The same body turned 30 degrees about y bakes by
     RESAMPLE: count exact, max shift <= 1.5 voxels, mean <= 0.6 voxel.
  R4 pop on the blast (CPU, over every bake in R1a): >= 80 % of bakes rigid (pieces come to rest on a face); per-voxel
     shift mean <= 0.5 voxel (6.25 cm), max <= 1.5 voxels (18.75 cm). Bodies woken by a bake move <= 1 voxel before
     sleeping again.
  R5 determinism: R1a twice -> identical world grids (hash) and identical bake order.
  R6 remove is clean: two bodies far apart, one falling, the other asleep; removing the sleeper leaves the faller's
     trajectory bit-equal to a run without removal.
  R7 cost: planning + applying the bake of all ~950 large-wall bodies at their extraction poses (rigid path) <= 10 ms
     total; at random rotations (resample path) <= 40 ms; the policy pass with nothing eligible, 950 bodies,
     <= 0.05 ms.

PREDICTIONS (live, L4, Release; project VoxFoundBodies, the F5.3 rig and camera; GPU sharing stated at run time)
  L1 the pop, near: the stone wall blast settled (900 ticks), bodies vs baked (budget 0, keep 0), the F5.3 side
     camera (~6 m): 2-30 % of the box's pixels differ above the +-1 control floor -- the shift is <= 6 cm and the

  L2 the pop, far: the same, the camera 4x farther along the same ray: <= 1/8 of the near pop's pixel count.
  L3 conservation live: the route's per-material world + bodies + crumbs counts equal before and after the bake.
  L4 cost: the stone wall's bake <= 5 ms CPU (plan + apply + face compaction); the preview rebuild after it measured
     and stated (today's path, not a new cost).
  L5 eye check: before / after pictures to the owner.
  L6 chunks: the lab is one area; a bake writes world cells by world position; nothing per chunk can show (stated).

WHAT WOULD COUNT AS A MISS
  any conservation difference; a baked cell overwriting solid; a near / awake / recent body baked; a rigid bake whose
  shape differs; any number above outside its stated range.
