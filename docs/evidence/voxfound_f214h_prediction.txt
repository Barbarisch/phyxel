F2.14h prediction (written 2026-11-14 before any code): the coarse canopy's shape vs the cards (§4.6 item 7
continued; the last look gap of the leaf-mass handoff).

Design: build {"lod_leaf_fill":t} (0 = off, the F2.14c/f mass). With t > 0 the leaf mass at levels >= h comes from the
LEAF pyramid (F2.14g's lodSplitCards, leaf voxels only), thresholded: a cell is leaf mass when its leaf count >= t x
8^k (lodLeafMass; material = its surface vote, else its volume vote, else the fallback leaf material). The solid
geometry stays the default one pyramid (cards hide their neighbours). {"lod_alt":{...}} builds a second variant for
an interleaved A/B ({"lod_alt_on":bool} swaps it).

1. Unit (red first): lodLeafMass on a rig -- a cell with 3/8 leaf cover is mass at t = 1/4, air at t = 1/2; counts
   exact; a cell whose leaves touch no air takes the volume vote or the fallback. Red on a stub.
2. Gate vs the cards (F2.14f rig: h=2, lod_levels 3, P=8, today's world hidden, wind 0, GI off; A = sprigs everywhere,
   B0 = t 0 mass, B = t mass), t in {1/2, 1/4, 1/8}: top_down px changed vs A 24.3 % (t 0) -> t 1/2 ~same (+-3),
   t 1/4 15-20 %, t 1/8 12-18 % (fatter fills the gaps, then overshoots the card outline); overview_south 10.9 % ->
   6-9 % at the best t. Luma stays within +-6 of A. Coverage lost px (background) stays ~0; gained (fattening against
   the sky) rises with lower t: <= 2,000 px at t 1/8.
3. Faces at levels 2 / 3 (h=2): t 1/2 ~= F2.14c's 142k / 31k (+-20 %); t 1/4 x1.3-1.8; t 1/8 x1.6-2.5.
4. Whole frame at the chosen t vs t 0, P=8: |delta| <= 0.3 ms from above; ground poses +0..+0.5 ms (more mass faces).
