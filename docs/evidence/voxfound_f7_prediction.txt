THE CHANGE: VoxFound::WorldReads answers the four on the new store (BlockStore per engine chunk, 256^3 voxels = 32 m;
one 8^3 block = one old 1 m cube), with the OLD semantics restated on 1/8 m voxels:
  - getVoxelTypeAt(cube): its block has no solid voxel -> EMPTY, all 512 -> CUBE, else SUBDIVIDED (O(1): the directory
    entry, or a detailed block's solid-mask popcount -- BlockStore::blockSolidCount, new); hasVoxelAt = not EMPTY.
  - subVoxelFloor(cube): empty -> 0; full -> solid (-1); else full horizontal voxel layers from the bottom, all content
    in them -> layers / 8, otherwise solid (-1) -- the old rule with 8 layers instead of 3.
  - occupiedMicro(micro 1/9 m): the 1/8 m voxel under the micro cell's centre is solid (1/9 and 1/8 m content can't
    coincide; a stated sampling).
  - a chunk the store does not have reads empty / 0 / false (as an unloaded chunk today).
  ChunkManager::setVoxFoundReads(reads): when set, the four route to it (consumers unchanged); NPCManager::buildNavGrid
  takes its bounds from the reads' chunk list when routed (a chunkMap walker, plan section 7 step 7, done for nav now).
  R1: every cube of a chunk of mixed content (uniform air / solid blocks, detailed blocks: full, partial, one voxel,
      slabs, random) -- getVoxelTypeAt / hasVoxelAt equal the brute-force rule on a dense VoxelGrid of the same content,
      including negative chunk coordinates and a chunk the store lacks.
  R2: subVoxelFloor: slabs of 1..7 layers = k/8; a slab with a voxel above, a block with no bottom layer, a full block ->
      -1; empty -> 0; and equal to the brute-force rule on every cube of R1's chunk.
  R3: occupiedMicro equals the dense voxel under each micro centre for every micro cell of a 4 x 4 x 4 m area.
  N1: navigation unchanged: NavGraphMicroTest's framed door rebuilt in 1/8 m voxels (wall 5 voxels thick, jambs one
      voxel, the reveal 6 voxels = 0.75 m wide x 2 m tall, the lintel above) in the new store; a ChunkManager with NO old
      chunks routed to it; NPCManager::buildNavGrid -> the path crosses the wall through the door; TEETH: the same wall
      without the reveal blocks. Control: the same ChunkManager not routed -> no nav (no chunks).
  R6: cost: getVoxelTypeAt <= 50 ns per call, subVoxelFloor on a detailed block <= 2 us (CPU, Release).
