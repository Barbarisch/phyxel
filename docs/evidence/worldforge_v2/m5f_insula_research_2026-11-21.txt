A. INSULA (CITY BLOCK) SIZE
  - Pompeii VIII.4: block length 75.72 m; Via dei Teatri 6.11 m; Via Stabiana 7.37 m. pompeiiperspectives.org
    (regio-viii-insula-4). PAGE-READ.
  - Pompeii other block lengths 33.52 / 33.83 / 72.71 / 43.85 m (III.1, I.9, VII.1, IX.11). Same site. SNIPPET.
  - Pompeii IX.3: "length ca. 60 m and width ca. 50 m", 3000-3500 m2. Univ. Helsinki Pompeii project
    (blogs.helsinki.fi/pompeii-project/?p=18). PAGE-READ.
  - Timgad: square insulae 20.65 x 20.45 to 21.8 x 21.3 m; rectangular 28.65 x 20.8 and 28.25 x 20.8 m. Carthage
    block 35.28 x 141.12 m (120 x 480 ft). Silchester Insula IV module 39.83 m. Britannia, "Length-units in Roman
    Town Planning: the pes monetalis and the pes Drusianus", fn 6, 10, 12, 16 (citing Boeswillwald, Cagnat &
    Ballu 1905). PAGE-READ (extract and footnotes; no author or year on the page).
  - Corinth: 1 actus (about 35.5 m) wide, 1-4 long, most often 1 x 2; measured width 35.486 m. Corinth Computer
    Project. SNIPPET (both URLs failed).
  READ: no canonical size. About 20 m (Timgad) to 35 x 70 m (actus grids) to Pompeii's irregular 34-76 m. Picking
  one for a generated town is a DESIGN pick inside a sourced range.

B. STREET AND PAVEMENT WIDTHS (TOWN)
  - Pompeii streets 6.11 m and 7.37 m (as above). PAGE-READ.
  - Twelve Tables VII.6: 8 ft straight, 16 ft on a bend (about 2.4 / 4.7 m). Yale Avalon. PAGE-READ; fragmentary,
    5th-century BC Rome - low as an applied value.
  - Pompeii streets 2-4 m typical, colonnaded about 14.8 m (AJA review of Poehler 2017, The Traffic Systems of
    Pompeii). SNIPPET.
  - Kerb height average 34.5 cm, least 8 cm (Van Roggen 2015 p. 63 via Poehler, AJA 122.4, 2018). SNIPPET.
  - Passable sidewalk about 1.06-1.71 m (Hartnett, AJA 112.1, 2008). SNIPPET (403).
  NO SOURCE: any Ostia street width.

C. TABERNA (SHOP)
  - Ostia: the mezzanine "usually at a height of three-and-a-half to four metres"; shops "almost always large and
    rectangular". ostia-antica.org shops intro. PAGE-READ. No width or depth.
  - Pompeii bars, type A.2: average floor area 44 m2 (25 to 78 m2). Ellis, Internet Archaeology 24, 6.1.3.
    PAGE-READ - floor AREA, bars only.
  - One shop threshold about 3.7 m with a 0.7 m inset doorway (Oxford ORA thesis, not confirmed). SNIPPET.
  READ: NO taberna width or depth with a stated source.

D. STOREY AND BUILDING HEIGHT
  - Augustus: no structure on the public streets "as high as seventy feet" (about 20.7 m). Strabo 5.3.7 (Loeb
    1923, Thayer). PAGE-READ.
  - A later limit of about 60 ft (17.75 m), attributed to Trajan - disputed (the plan's D68 already says so).
    Wikipedia "Insula (building)", secondary. PAGE-READ.
  - An Ostia building about 17.70 m over four floors "according to Stevens" (BABesch 80, 2005) - two sites attach
    it to different buildings. SNIPPET; check Stevens directly.
  - Packer 1971 (via a snippet): doors about 2.00 x 1.00 m, windows 1.50-2.00 m high x 0.90-1.30 m wide. SNIPPET.

E. PARTY AND WALL THICKNESS
  - Vitruvius 2.8.17: public laws forbid walls on a public way thicker than "one foot and a half" (about 0.44 m);
    such a wall carries one floor. Thayer. PAGE-READ for the first sentence; the "2-3 bricks for more storeys"
    detail from a search summary. This VERIFIES the 1.5 pedes the plan's D68 held "from memory".
  - Standing walls 0.5-0.7 m (Straumann 2011, already read).
