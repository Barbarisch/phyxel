   hydrology bake's height = WorldGenerator::baseHeightFn() at the cell centre = the meso sheet +

2. WorldGenerator::sampleColumn (WorldGenerator.cpp:1386-1390): within kValleyWidthMul (5) x the
   channel half-width of a carved channel the Layer-1 relief is multiplied by
   smoothstep(0, valleyHalf, dist) - a FRACTION of the relief, 0 at the centreline - so the land
   beside the channel is coarse.baseHeight (+ the biome offset) while the profile was sampled with
   the full relief on. On the Mountains world the relief is 100-300 u, so:
   (a) EMBANKMENT: where the profile (full relief - depth) stands above the attenuated land the
       M2d-1 rule fills the channel columns up to the profile (line 1468-1471: "filled where it
       dips below") and the channel runs on a wall - the M4c dumps: a channel at 236 u between
       banks at 72/98, at 170 between 74/85, at 45 between 12/14 (m4c_red_green.txt lines 806+).
   (b) SLOT CANYON: the fractional attenuation drives 70-100 u of relief to 0 over the 5 half-

       .87 .72 .72 .57 .53 42 then the channel at 24-30). The creek-swale comment at line 1397
       already records this failure mode for creeks ("a fraction is only safe over a wide valley")
       and moved creeks to an ABSOLUTE depth; rivers kept the fraction.
   The two are one defect: the channel section is not referenced to the land beside it and the
   valley side has no bounded slope.

=== GROUNDING PASS (grounding-auditor, 2026-10-29 20:21, the numbers the design carries; rows added to plan section 10) ===
  Valley-side slope bound 0.70 (35 deg): REASONED - the top of the 30 +/- 5 deg threshold-hillslope
    band (Frontiers Earth Sci. 2021, 10.3389/feart.2021.684365, read: 82 % of slopes inside it,
    basin modes 26-33 deg) and equal to the GROUNDED scree repose the roads use; Burbank 1996's
    abstract gives NO angle (the "32 deg" is unverified - the Nature paper unread); 0.58 (30 deg)
    would be the modal value.
  Residual embankment <= 1 u: GROUNDED-conservative - natural levees "a few centimeters up to 3 m",
    modelled 0.4-2.1 m, ~20 % of bankfull depth (PMC7780550, read); tens of metres only on the
    Amazon / Yellow / Mekong. 100-250 u embankments are outside every source.
  Flat floor at the bankfull rim across 5 x width: REASONED (a floodplain is a flat landform;
    NetMap: ~2 x bankfull depth above the channel); the transition and the 5 x multiple stay
    NEEDS-RESEARCH as the plan already records.
  Gorge side slope: NEEDS-RESEARCH - cohesive bedrock "should support steeper slopes" (Larsen &
    Montgomery 2012, snippet, unread), no number; the rule lets a natural cliff stand (the
    natural wins), no steeper allowance without a number.
  Auditor's verdict: the rule is acceptable to ship with S flagged REASONED; the mismatch it fixes
  (10-64 u per column walls, 100-250 u embankments) is far outside every source read.
