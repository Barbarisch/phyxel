WHAT IS OPEN: O39 (f) let a walker step over a crest between two cells that are a STEP apart. Where the far cell is a
  FALL below (more than the step-up, within the fall limit) the old rule still stands: on the near cell's columns
  the body must fit exactly at the near level, and on the far cell's columns the whole span from the landing up to
  the near level + the body must be clear. A hump one micro high at the cell boundary - or the near cell's ground
  running one column into the far cell - refuses the edge. A character walks off such a lip.
THE RULE: a fall edge is walked in two phases along its 9 columns - STANDING, then FALLING, never back.
  standing: the body fits at some level from the near cell's level up to ONE step-up above it (the lowest such level;
    the lift is remembered) - on the near cell's columns, and on the far cell's columns while the ground there is
    still at or above the near level (a lip or a crest).
  falling: from the first far-cell column where the body fits at the landing level, the span from the landing up to
    the highest level stood on + the body must be clear, on that column and every one after it.
  bounds: the lift is at most the step-up above the NEAR (higher) cell; the drop from the highest level stood on to
    the landing is at most the fall limit. An intermediate ledge BELOW the near level inside the far cell is still
    refused (said here; a staircase within one cell is not this change).
  No new number: the step-up and the fall limit are the agent's own.
