               inflating toward a lump.

               anywhere in a brick blanks the WHOLE brick. This is the PRICE of
               preserving openings, and it dominates: at 16^3 a single doorway
               erases 4096 cubes of wall.
mat_mismatch = skin-material drift, ignoring carved openings. INSENSITIVE on this
               structure (only 2 materials). Recorded, not used to decide.
units/chunk  = (32/brick)^3, the cull-unit multiplier vs one-draw-per-chunk today.

Authored opening hints ARE fed here: AssemblyPlan::openings -> preserveOpening,
using only role "clear" reveal boxes (jamb/lintel/sill are solid trim, not void).
Two earlier metrics were discarded as vacuous and must not be reinstated: a
bbox-relative "hollowness" (inflated purely from bbox rounding -- read 50% at
16^3 where the whole building is ONE brick), and "openings retained"
(tautologically 100% at every size, since OrPreserveOpenings is DEFINED to let an
opening win). Neither discriminated. Over-carve does.
True pop VISIBILITY needs the C4 renderer and is NOT measured here.
