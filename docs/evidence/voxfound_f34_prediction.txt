F3.4 -- the converter from PXB2 (VoxelFoundationPlan §3.6, §7a, §9b). Written 2026-11-16, BEFORE any code.

§3.6: "Old worlds: a converter from PXB2. A cube converts exactly to one 8x8x8 block. Subcubes and microcubes don't
land on the 1/8 m grid and are resampled (§7a). Generated worlds are regenerated instead, which is exact."
§7a step 7 names the resampling rule: volume per material within one voxel.

PXB2 (ChunkBlobCodec v1/v2): palette (entry 0 air, u16 indices when > 256 entries), the 32^3 cubes RLE (z-minor), then
subcubes (u16 cube index, u8 slot x + 3y + 9z, index, state, [tint]) and microcubes (+ u8 slot inside the subcube),
then (v2) water spans. "Visible" = present: interior cubes are stored.

Change:
1. VoxFound::convertPxb2(blob) -> BlockStore 256^3 (+ the blob's material names): a present cube -> its 8^3 block
   written whole (exact). A cube cell holding subcubes / microcubes: its 9^3 ninth grid (a subcube fills 3^3 ninths,
   a microcube one), resampled to 8^3 -- each 1/8 m voxel takes the material with the most volume inside it, air
   counted as a material; ties go to the solid material, then to the lower blob palette index. Dropped and counted,
   not silently lost: tint, state (v3 has no channels yet: §3.6 "optional channels follow"), water spans (the water
   runtime keeps its own). Malformed / truncated / unknown-version blobs refused.
2. Region build {"source":"world_db"}: the region's chunks come from the open world.db's PXB2 blobs through the
   converter instead of the generator (border planes from the converted neighbours; a missing neighbour = air),
   faces built by FaceBuilderGpu as for a generated region; editable as usual.

Red first (CPU):
- VoxFoundStorageLab.ConverterCubesAreExact: today's Chunk with random cubes of several materials (and one whole
  uniform chunk) -> ChunkBlobCodec::encode -> convert: every voxel = the cube it lies in; every cube a uniform block.
- VoxFoundStorageLab.ConverterResamplesByVolume: Chunks with random subcubes and microcubes (incl. a cube cell whose
  27 subcubes are one material, and cells mixing subcubes and microcubes of different materials) -> PXB2 -> convert:
  equal voxel for voxel to a reference written separately in the test (volumes in doubles from the content list,
  same tie rule); a cell fully subdivided into one material converts to a uniform block; tint / state / water counted.
- VoxFoundStorageLab.ConverterRejectsMalformedPxb2: every truncation of a real blob, wrong magic / version.
All red on a stub.

Predictions (live: CityBench_C25, 64 saved chunks, 25 buildings; 1.33 M cubes, 119 k subcubes, 1.32 M microcubes):
1. Every cube converts exactly: converted solid volume from cubes = cube count x 512 voxels (counted per chunk).
2. Volume per material, converted vs PXB2 (cubes + subcubes/27 + microcubes/729 m^3): within +-1 % for the materials
   carried mostly by cubes; materials carried by microcube detail (furniture, trim) within +-25 % (majority votes of
   1/9 m features at 1/8 m; thin one-ninth features can vanish or fatten) -- measured and reported per material.
3. Convert time 5-80 ms per chunk (cube runs as whole blocks; 10^4 subdivided cells in the busiest chunks).
4. Pixels, today's world vs the converted region at 3 poses near buildings (§4.3 pass: debug tonemap, wind 0, GI off,
   3D viewport, > 8/255, controls each side): cube-only surfaces (walls, ground, roofs on whole metres) differ little;
   the differences concentrate on subcube / microcube detail (shape changes by up to a voxel) and on content each
   renderer draws differently (F2.19: grass, leaf cards). No numeric threshold in the plan; each cluster of
   differing pixels attributed in the diff map.

---- 2026-11-16 interim: run 2 of the live gate, then a rule added (written before the code below) ----
Measured (run 2, voxfound_f34_gate.txt before this addendum; run 1 kept in f34_run1_badposes/: two of its poses sat in
the grass / looked into the void): cube surfaces identical by eye and in the diff maps (stone walls, streets, building
walls, the crenellated wall); differences = leaf bushes, grass speckle (today's blades), roof course lines (thirds on
eighths). Volume per material: Stone / Dirt / Grass / StoneBricks within 0.3 %, but thin detail lost: LogBirch -86 %,
glow -43 % (lamps: light sources), Metal -35 %. Found by eye: a planter's leaves (4 Leaf microcubes per metre cell, 4/729
of it) vanish entirely -- every one of those ninths straddles voxel edges and loses the vote to air.
The plan already has the rule for this: §7a step 4 (items), "No detail vanishes: a material+tint the content rule
dropped everywhere recolours the cell at its centre; if that cell holds the last cell of another detail, it takes the
nearest empty face-neighbour instead". Applied per cube cell in the converter:
  after the vote, every material present in the cell's ninths with no voxel left (air excepted) takes the voxel where
  it has the most volume (ties: lower x, then y, then z); a voxel that is the LAST voxel of another material in the
  cell, or that another rescued material already took, is skipped for the next-best one. Materials in ascending blob
  palette order. Counted (rescued).
Red: ConverterResamplesByVolume's reference gets the same rule (written apart) + a new
ConverterKeepsEveryMaterialOfACell (isolated single microcubes of several materials in otherwise air / stone cells:
every material present in a cell is present after the conversion). Red on the vote alone.
Predictions: every material keeps >= 1 voxel per cell it was in; volume error of the trims shrinks (glow, Metal,
LogBirch within -50..+100 %: a 1/729 m^3 microcube becomes a 1/512 m^3 voxel, +42 % where it was alone); the big
materials unchanged within 0.3 %. The planter's leaves come back as voxels; whether they DRAW is the sprig model's
density rule (F2.4: ~4.5 sprigs per m^2 of exposed leaf, about 7 % of exposed cells), not the converter's -- measured
and reported, not changed here.
