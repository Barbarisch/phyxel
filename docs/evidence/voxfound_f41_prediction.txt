THE MODEL (fixed here, before the first run; lengths in metres, so the same rules run on world voxels
1/8 m and item voxels 1/64 m -- §6.8)

  e = E / T of the material at the hit (the first solid voxel along the hit direction).
  Stress at distance d (metres) from the hit:  s(d) = E / (1 + (d / l)^2),  l = 0.25 m.
  A bond (two face-adjacent solid voxels) has strength
     S = T_min(a, b) * k * (1 - 0.5 * max(damage_a, damage_b) / 255)
     k = partBondScale (0.3)           if the two voxels have different part ids     (mortar, glue)
     k = 1 + 3 * |bond axis . grain|  otherwise (fibrous: a bond across the fibres is 4x stronger)
  A bond is a CANDIDATE when it lies on a weak plane:
     - a part or material boundary, or
     - a boundary of the crack pattern at the local level (below).
  A candidate bond is CUT when s(d_bond) >= S. Nothing else is ever cut.

  The crack pattern (§6.4):
   - brittle + fibrous: intersection of jittered-grid Voronoi partitions at levels k = 0..L, spacing
     1 m / 2^k, seeds hashed from (hit seed, level, absolute grid cell). Local cell size
       h(d) = 0.5 m * (1 + d / l) / cbrt(e)          (small near the hit, larger away; shrinks with energy)
     and the bond's level L = floor(log2(1 m / h(d))) clamped to [0, finest level >= one voxel].
     Fibrous: distances along the grain are compressed 4x, so cells are 4x longer along the grain.
   - glass: polar cells about the hit in the plane across the hit direction: n = round(4 cbrt(e)) radial
     sectors (small per-ring wobble) x rings at r0 * 1.7^k, r0 = h(0) / 2.
   - parts: a part whose equivalent size cbrt(volume) is <= h at its centroid moves as a unit (the pattern
     does not enter it; its cracks run along its boundary = mortar / joints). Larger parts are cut by the
     pattern like plain material.
  Nesting (brittle / fibrous, no prior damage): raising e only lowers h and the thresholds, so
  cut(e) is a subset of cut(e') for e < e' -> loose matter never decreases with energy. Glass is not nested.

  Pieces: union-find over uncut bonds. Anchored = the piece holds a fixed voxel. Loose pieces: crumbs when
  volume < 4 world voxels (4 / 512 m^3; a tier knob, it never changes which voxels are loose), else bodies.
  Damage: every solid voxel gains round(255 * min(1, s(d) / T)) (saturating), cut or not.
